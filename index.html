About Me

I’m a product designer who uses a multidisciplinary approach that blends systems thinking, creative problem-solving, and hands-on prototyping to design products that address real human needs.

I graduated from Northwestern University with a B.S. in Manufacturing & Design Engineering (MaDE), with minors in Computer Science and Art Theory & Practice. I entered Northwestern intending to become a physical product designer, but through my coursework and projects, I discovered a passion for creating memorable digital experiences that deliver meaningful value to users.

I work at the intersection of product design, engineering, and visual communication. I bring both creative thinking and engineering rigor to every project. Throughout the design process, my goal is to create thoughtful, user-centered solutions that are both functional and impactful.

I'm from Queens (the best borough in New York City). To unwind, I enjoy listening to a wide range of music and painting my favorite album covers. I'm also a passionate Mets and Knicks fan and an avid cook who loves experimenting in the kitchen.
